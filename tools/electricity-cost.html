Electricity cost calculator

How much does a TV, heater, fan or other appliance cost to run? Enter its wattage and the rate from your electricity bill. All calculations happen in your browser.

Estimated monthly electricity cost—

How the math works

(Watts ÷ 1,000) × hours per day × days per month = kWh per month. Multiply kWh by your rate in dollars per kWh to estimate energy cost. For instance, a device averaging 100 W for eight hours daily over 30 days uses 24 kWh. At $0.15/kWh that is $3.60.

What this does not include

Electricity bills can contain additional fixed fees, taxes and tiered rates. A nameplate watt rating may be a maximum; actual consumption changes with usage cycles and device settings. Check your bill for the appropriate rate and use a power meter if you need a measured average.
