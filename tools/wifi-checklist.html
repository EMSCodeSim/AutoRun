Wi-Fi acting up?

Check whether the issue is the internet connection, the Wi-Fi signal or one device. Work through these checks in order. No personal data leaves this page.

Step-by-step checklist

  1. If just one device is affected, focus on that device's network settings.

  2. Improvement nearby suggests signal strength, distance or interference may be involved.

  3. Do this before changing router settings, especially if every device is offline.

  4. Use the manufacturer's guide to interpret warning lights; do not touch damaged power cables.

  5. A restart is different from a factory reset. Avoid resetting settings unless you have backed them up.

  6. Record the symptoms, error messages and devices affected.

A useful diagnostic distinction

Wi-Fi is your local wireless connection; internet service connects your network to outside services. A device can show strong Wi-Fi yet have no usable internet connection.
