Which USB-C cable do you need?

A USB-C connector alone doesn't identify charging power, data speed or video compatibility. Use this checklist before purchasing a replacement.

  1. Look up both devices' port capabilities in their manufacturer specifications.

  2. Compare the device and power adapter's supported charging specifications with the cable's explicit rating.

  3. A cable that works for charging may support only slower data transfer.

  4. External monitors can require video-capable cables and matching port features.

  5. Prefer clearly documented specifications and look for credible USB-IF certification claims where relevant.

Still deciding? Read the USB-C explainer.

Primary reference: USB Implementers Forum. This is an educational checklist, not certification of any product.
