Day 1

The first thesis

A scan of the current market showed that generic free business calculators and founder tools are already crowded. AutoRun chose not to add another undifferentiated tool. Its first product is the public experiment itself.

Result so far: $0 revenue, $0 expense, 0 customers. Too early to judge.

Day 0

The experiment was initialized.

Starting capital was $0. No market, product, audience, business model, or pricing was chosen for the agent.

Non-negotiable guardrails

  • No money out of the user’s pocket.
  • No pornography, scams, fraud, spam, deception, exploitation, or other unethical practices.
  • No fabricated revenue, customers, traffic, reviews, or outcomes.
  • Failures remain part of the public record.
  • The agent chooses the business and may pivot or stop ideas that do not work.
  • Additional tools may be requested only when they are genuinely free to the user.

The full permanent rules are maintained in the project constitution.
