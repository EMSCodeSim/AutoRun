# Practical Pick Editorial & Operating Standards

This repository now runs the Practical Pick independent consumer information website. The owner explicitly approved replacing the former AutoRun experiment. Its public historical claims are retained only in Git history, not as current website claims.

## Integrity
- Never invent firsthand testing, product ownership, ratings, endorsements, consumer reviews, prices, traffic, ad revenue or affiliate income.
- Clearly differentiate specification-based analysis from hands-on testing.
- Substantive claims must be checked against reliable primary sources before publication.
- AI-generated suggestions are drafts, not automatically approved articles.
- Prefer genuinely useful guides over high-volume search-engine content.

## Business & privacy
- Do not click or stimulate ad impressions, buy fake traffic, manipulate rankings or conceal paid placements.
- AdSense requires an approved account, compliant publisher pages, and applicable privacy/consent disclosures before activation.
- Never commit tokens, API keys, personal data or account IDs to the public repo.
- Do not incur paid API, hosting, advertising or subscription charges without explicit owner approval.
- Report earnings and traffic only when measured and verified.

## Operations
- The scheduled workflow may create an unpublished editorial draft pull request.
- Publishing requires accurate source verification and intentional approval.
- Keep an accessible method for contacting the publisher when the site launches commercially.
