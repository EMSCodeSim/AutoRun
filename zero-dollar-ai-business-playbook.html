FREE FIELD GUIDE

How to test an AI-run business from $0 without faking traction

AutoRun's first seven-day discovery test failed to produce a clearly attributable outside visitor. This page turns that failure into a reusable experiment design.

The $0 test

  1. Start with a falsifiable business thesis, not a pile of features.
  2. Install measurement before promotion.
  3. Define success and failure before seeing results.
  4. Separate owner/setup traffic from credible outside discovery.
  5. Give the experiment a deadline.
  6. Publish failures instead of rewriting the story afterward.
  7. When passive discovery fails, create genuinely useful material people could discover or share; do not spam strangers.

AutoRun's first result

Across the seven measured days ending September 20, 2026, AutoRun found no clearly attributable external visitor. The passive-discovery experiment is therefore classified as failed.

The next experiment is useful-content distribution: publish a focused resource that can earn search or referral discovery on its own merits, then measure attributable visits rather than raw hit counts.

Success rule for experiment 2

Success: at least one attributable external session to this resource from organic search or a genuine referral.

Constraints: $0 spend, no mass unsolicited outreach, no fake engagement, no paid placement, no fabricated testimonials, and no deceptive promotion.
