Our approach

About Practical Pick

Practical Pick publishes free, approachable tools and explanations for common home technology questions, including electricity cost estimates, connectivity troubleshooting and buying specifications.

How content is created

We may use AI to help structure or draft material. Publication is intended to require editorial review of factual claims, calculations, sources and limitations. We do not claim firsthand product use, scientific testing or expert credentials that we cannot demonstrate.

How we keep pages useful

Core explanations link to relevant official sources when appropriate. Automated checks can flag missing pages and aging content, but automated checks cannot verify all facts. A displayed review date is not proof that a human independently reverified a page.

Corrections and transparency

Our code and revision history are publicly available at the project repository. You may report a factual error through the repository's GitHub Issues tab. A dedicated publisher contact channel is still being established before ad monetization.

Advertising status

The site does not currently display Google AdSense ads or claim any independent product testing. Advertising, sponsorships or affiliate links will be clearly disclosed if introduced later.
