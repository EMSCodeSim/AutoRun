Choosing an LED desk lamp

The best lamp for your desk depends on what you do, where you sit and how much ambient light the room already receives.

Think about positioning first

An adjustable arm or head can help direct light toward papers or a work surface instead of a screen. Measure the desk and check how a base or clamp will fit.

Look for usable controls

Stepped or continuous dimming can help when lighting conditions vary. Consider whether a lamp remembers your preferred settings and whether the controls are convenient to reach.

Consider glare and comfort

A diffused light source and flexible aiming may help minimize distracting reflections. Consider the position of windows, monitors and glossy surfaces before deciding how much light you need.

Check practical details

  • Desk space and reach of the lamp.
  • Brightness adjustability and orientation.
  • Power supply and replacement policy.
  • Warranty and credible manufacturer documentation.

Before you buy

Compare the measurements and documented specifications of a few models against your actual desk setup. We have not conducted hands-on product testing for this guide.
