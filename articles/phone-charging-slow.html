Why is my phone charging slowly?

A practical checklist for checking cables, adapters, heat and charging settings safely.

Published 2026-10-16 · Prepared with automated editorial assistance · Illustration is an original explanatory diagram, not a product photo or test result.

Separate normal slowdowns from faults

Many devices vary their charging speed throughout a charging session. Charging may slow as the battery approaches full, or when the device is warm. One measurement at an arbitrary battery percentage does not prove that a cable or adapter is defective.

If a device reports a charging or temperature warning, follow the device manufacturer's instructions before testing anything else. Stop using damaged, swollen, wet or unusually hot charging equipment.

Start with the power source

A computer USB port, a low-power adapter and a capable USB Power Delivery charger can offer very different charging capability. Check your phone's documented supported charging methods and compare them to the adapter specifications.

A higher number printed on a charger does not mean the phone will continuously draw that power. The phone, charger, cable and charging conditions all play a role.

Check the cable without guessing

USB-C describes connector shape, not a guarantee of particular current, transfer speed or charging protocol. Some cables are unsuitable for high-power or specialized uses. Read the printed rating or manufacturer specification rather than assuming all USB-C cables are identical.

Try a known-good manufacturer-compatible cable if one is available. Do not repair or continue using a cable with exposed conductors, melted insulation or damaged connectors.

Consider temperature, apps and settings

Charging can be affected by thermal management, wireless charging alignment and device settings. Heavy activities such as gaming or video recording may consume power while the device is charging and make the percentage rise more slowly.

If supported, check the device's battery health and optimized charging information. Device menus vary, so rely on the manufacturer manual for the exact setting names rather than following a generic sequence of taps.

When to seek service

Repeated unexpected shutdowns, excessive heat, physical battery damage or persistent charging errors warrant support from the device maker or an authorized service provider. Do not attempt to puncture or dismantle lithium-ion batteries.

For an ordinary slow-charge complaint, document the adapter, cable, battery level, temperature and approximate charge time before contacting support. Those details help avoid replacing perfectly good accessories.

Buying checklist

Before buying a new accessory, confirm the phone's supported input, charger output and the cable's verified rating. Compare a known-good setup first. Favor clear technical specifications and reliable return policies over unsupported speed claims.
