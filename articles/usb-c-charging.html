Understanding USB-C charging cables

The USB-C connector tells you the shape of the plug, not everything a cable can do. Different cables can support different charging power, transfer speeds and display features.

Start with the devices

Check the charging requirements of the laptop, phone or other device, as well as the charger. A cable does not increase the power a charger can supply.

Check the power rating

Some cables are designed for lower charging power and others support higher USB Power Delivery power levels. For higher-power use, look for an appropriately rated and clearly labeled cable from a reputable maker. Verify its specifications and any applicable certification claims.

Think about data

A USB-C cable used primarily for charging may not offer the transfer speed you expect for external drives. If you need video output, confirm the cable explicitly supports the required mode and that your devices do as well.

Use a buying checklist

  • Confirm both device and charger specifications.
  • Match the cable's supported power level.
  • Choose the speed and video capabilities you actually need.
  • Check practical length, strain relief and return policy.

Further reading
